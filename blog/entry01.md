## Choosing a topic

The topic that i decided on for the freedom project is **Sports** because i like to watch and play sports and i wanted to learn how teams and athletes use data and technology to win games and prevent injuries. I have always found it interesting how some decisions and tactics can change the outcome of a game. I wanted to learn how coaches use wearable trackers, AI video analysis to optimize training for their players to make them play better. Some of my favorite articles about this topic include The ["Impact of Emerging Technology in Sports"](https://www.ohio.edu/business/academics/graduate/professional-master-sports-administration/resources/technology-sports) by Ohio University, ["What is Sports Technology](https://www.globant.com/tech-terms/sports-technology) by Globant, and [Technology in Sports](https://www.ebsco.com/research-starters/sports-and-leisure/technology-sports/) by EBSCO. All the articles were very informative and they made me want to learn even more about how technology was used in sports and how it helps the athletes.
